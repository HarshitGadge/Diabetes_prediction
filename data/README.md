# Data

Pima Indians Diabetes dataset (National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases; UCI / Kaggle):
768 rows × 9 columns, `Outcome` = 1 for diabetes (268 cases).

Download `diabetes.csv` (e.g. from Kaggle "Pima Indians Diabetes Database", or the public copy at
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/plotly/datasets/master/diabetes.csv) and place it here as `data/diabetes.csv`.
